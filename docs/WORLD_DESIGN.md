# World Design — BHARAT: BEYOND THE STREETS

## Regions (original, fictional, India-inspired)
1. Metro City — modern skyline, apartments, commercial, flyovers, metro, markets, malls, hospitals, parks, universities, police stations
2. Old City — dense streets, traditional architecture, street food, courtyards, historic landmarks
3. Rural India — villages, farms, fields, rural roads, tractor routes, wells
4. Desert Region — desert terrain, sand roads, desert settlements, fort structures, long routes
5. Mountain Region — mountain roads, hill villages, forests, tunnels, scenic overlooks
6. Coastal Region — beaches, coastal towns, harbor, fishing, coastal roads
7. Forest Region — forests, nature trails, rural settlements, wildlife, campsites
8. Festival City — events, changing celebrations

## World Simulation
- NPCs with daily schedules (walk, sit, shop, work, talk, travel, eat, attend events)
- Traffic scaled by distance (full near, reduced medium, none far)
- Region streaming + chunk loading + LOD + occlusion culling
- No single enormous scene on Android
